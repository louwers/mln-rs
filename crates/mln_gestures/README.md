# mln-rs-gestures

> **Warning: this is an internal crate of
> [mln-rs](https://crates.io/crates/mln-rs).** It is published only because
> mln-rs depends on it, and its API can change in any release without notice.
> Depend on [`mln-rs`](https://crates.io/crates/mln-rs) instead: it re-exports
> this crate as `mln_rs::mln_gestures`.

Turns pointer, touch, wheel, platform pinch and keyboard input into camera moves
and gesture momentum, for hosts that have no gesture recognizer of their own
(the desktop app, the browser build). MapLibre Native has no counterpart: its
platform SDKs each recognize gestures. This one is ported from
[Orbis](https://github.com/sargunv/orbis)'s `orb-camera` input, and drives the
map through maplibre-native-ffi's camera calls (`Map::apply_camera_delta` and
`Map::update_camera`, with their gesture phases), which mln-rs implements in its
core.

## Where each mechanism comes from

The gesture set is MapLibre Compose's. Each mechanism takes the best of the
references, in this order:

1. Measurements of the native map apps and platform scrolling, where someone has
   made them: maplibre/maplibre-native-ffi#1705 measured Apple Maps', Google
   Maps', UIScrollView's and Android's glides, and ffi's C API defaults follow
   them.
2. maplibre-native-ffi's C API, the contract that mln-rs, Orbis and Compose
   converge on, and its browser demo, which feels right.
3. Compose, Orbis and GL JS where they do better than both, or have the
   mechanism alone. Each such row says why.

Sources were read at: ffi `9cbe0b9` (`include/maplibre_native_c`,
`src/map/map.cpp`, `examples/browser/src/demo.ts`), Orbis `bef8eda`
(`crates/orb-camera`, `web/src/input.ts`), maplibre-compose `5b6fb4e` with
Compose UI 1.12.1 for the web (`WebVelocityTracker`,
`PointerVelocityTracker1D`), and GL JS v5.13.0 (`src/ui/handler*`,
`src/ui/camera.ts`). n/a: the reference has no such mechanism; unverified: not
read in its source. One row names WhirlyGlobe-Maply, read at `b836eaa`
(`GlobePanDelegate.mm`).

## Measuring the provisional zoom rates

The notch amount (⅓ level) and the trackpad rate (1/300 level per px) are ffi's
demo's and Compose's, not measurements: maplibre/maplibre-native-ffi#1705
measured the native apps' glides, not their scroll zoom. To measure them as it
did, record Apple Maps (macOS) and Google Maps (web, and on each platform)
zooming at a fixed point, for single notches of a mouse wheel and for a trackpad
scroll of known distance, read the zoom from the scale bar or the URL, and fit
the amount per notch, its easing duration, and the levels per point of trackpad
travel.

Part of [mln-rs](https://github.com/louwers/mln-rs), a Rust port of the
[MapLibre Native](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-native) map rendering
core. All its crates are released together, at the same version.
